Will It Fit

Privacy

Effective 22 August 2026 · applies to the website and the iOS app

The short version: Will It Fit has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your floor plans and furniture boards never leave your device. The only thing ever shared is anonymous product size data that helps the next person.

What stays on your device

Your boards — traced floor plans, furniture placements, saved furniture, settings — are stored in your browser's local storage (or the app's equivalent) on your device only. We run no server that receives, stores or can see them. Clearing your browser data (or deleting the app) deletes them.

When you paste a listing link

The listing page and its floor plan image are fetched through our own relay (a Cloudflare Worker) or public fetch services, because property portals don't allow browsers to read them directly. The link is used only to fetch the page at that moment. Standard, short-lived infrastructure logs aside, we don't record which listings you look at.

The community size cache

When a furniture product's dimensions are successfully read (from a shop link, the bookmarklet, the share sheet or pasted text), the product's link, name, dimensions, price and product photo URL may be saved to a shared cache so the next person pasting the same product gets an instant answer. Nothing about you is attached — no identifier, no account, no device information. Entries expire automatically after 180 days.

The iOS app and share sheet

The "Add to Will It Fit" share extension reads the page you're sharing on your device, extracts the product name, link and size text, and hands it to the app. It is processed exactly like a pasted product and feeds the same community cache described above — nothing else is collected.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare runs our fetch relay and the community cache.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this page and the site.

We sell nothing to anyone. There is nothing to sell.
