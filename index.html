How it works

Three steps from listing to layout

No measuring tape, no graph paper. The numbers come from the agent’s floor plan; the judgement stays with you.

  1. 01

    Paste the link

    Any Rightmove, Zoopla or OnTheMarket listing with a floor plan. No account, nothing to install.

  2. 02

    We read the plan

    Your browser fetches the listing, finds the agent’s floor plan and traces the walls, rooms and doors — scaled from the quoted floor area, so 3.60 m on the plan is 3.60 m on your board.

  3. 03

    Furnish and share

    Drag real-size furniture, see the gap to every wall, get warned when a door won’t open — then send the board to whoever you’re deciding with.

What you can do

Built for the questions you actually ask on a viewing

Will the bed fit — and which way?

Drop in a king bed and the board shows the gap to every wall. Turn it with one key and compare.

Does the door still open?

Every door swings on the board. Park a sofa in its arc and you’ll be told, before the removal van does.

How big is it, really?

Measure any two points, switch between metres and feet, and type in the exact size of the sofa you’re eyeing up.

Mind the eaves

Low-headroom zones and fixed fittings are marked, so the wardrobe doesn’t end up where you can’t stand.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your board is saved in your own browser. Share it with a board code or a link — the other person doesn’t need an account either.

How accurate are the room sizes?

We scale the agent’s plan from the dimensions they quote, and it usually agrees to within a few centimetres. Agents’ plans are “for identification only”, so for anything tight, measure on the viewing.

What if the listing has no floor plan?

Upload a photo or scan of one and we’ll auto-trace it. If the drawing is too unusual to trace, it stays as a background image: set the scale with two clicks and trace the rooms in a minute.

Which portals work?

Rightmove and Zoopla read automatically — we fetch the listing in your browser, find the floor plan and trace it on the spot. Other portals are hit and miss. If a link won’t read (portals do sometimes block), upload the floor plan image and we trace that instead.

Is my data private?

Yes. The plan and your furniture live in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a board code or save into a shared page.

Can two of us work on the same board?

Yes — swap board codes, or save into a shared page if you’re using one. Live co-editing is on the list.
